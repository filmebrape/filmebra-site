WORK

We are a Peruvian social film production company that has spent nearly a decade working on stories that media does not cover and institutions ignore.

We work with communities in crisis, disputed territories, and identities without coverage, telling the story of those who build it from within, of what is lost when a community loses its territory, and what it gains when it reclaims its voice.

Every production is grounded in research and multilateral dialogue, with scripts that make truth more powerful than any other form of entertainment.
